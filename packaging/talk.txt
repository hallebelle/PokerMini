# POKER MINI - talk.txt: the CHAT and TEASE exchanges in her room (and what she says around them).
# Edit with Notepad (plain text). Keep the name talk.txt next to the game. The full guide is TEXT_GUIDE.txt.
# An exchange:   > what you say      < her answer      < her second line (optional)      then a blank line.
# You can add as many exchanges to a topic as you like. The topics themselves (poker, weather, ...) are fixed.
# Anything the game refuses is skipped and written to text_log.txt; the built-in text is used instead. Delete this file to get a fresh copy.

# CHAT topic: poker
# > = what you say, < = her answer, second < = her second line (optional); a blank line between exchanges; each text: one dialogue page, 3 lines of 46 characters at most
[C_POKER]
> Teach me one thing about reading people at the table.
< Watch the hands. Liars touch their faces, nervous people touch their chips. You touch both. Constantly.
< ...It's adorable how bad you are.

< Never. A queen knows exactly what she holds. You think a pair of fours is a miracle.

> What's the worst beat you ever took?
< ...Don't. Pocket aces, and some idiot caught a two-outer on the river. I still remember his smile.
< I let him leave with his teeth, so he should be grateful.

> Is poker skill or luck?
< Luck decides one hand. Skill decides a thousand. That's why I win and you pray.

> Do you play online too?
< A screen can't see a face. Where's the fun in beating someone who can't blush?

> What was your first game like?
< Pennies and a deck missing the jack of clubs. I won everything. The grown-ups cried.
< I've been unbeatable ever since. Mostly.

> Would you ever let me win on purpose?
< Hmph. A queen does not gift victories.
< ...But if you play well, maybe I'll lose by accident.

> Which starting hand is your favorite?
< Queens, naturally. Pocket queens look at the whole table and say: kneel.

> Any advice before our next match?
< Fold more. Talk less. And stop looking at me when you should be looking at your cards.

> How do you stay so calm when you're losing?
< I don't lose. And if it ever happens, I smile, so everyone is too scared to enjoy it.

> Have you ever folded a winning hand?
< Once. I read him perfectly and I was still wrong. It's the only time I enjoyed being wrong.
